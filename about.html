About AgriNote

Smart Farming Assistant · by PRIME Developer

What is AgriNote?

AgriNote is a smart farming diary for Android. It helps you keep every crop, expense and sale in one place, so you always know how your farm is doing.

What you can do

  • Add your crops with area in acres and sowing date, and see the days left or passed.
  • Log expenses under each crop.
  • Record every sale with the market, date and amount.
  • See expenses, income and net profit for each crop at a glance.
  • Open Analytics for financial trends, income and expense breakdowns, and a crop-wise profit comparison.
  • Choose from 6 themes and gentle effects like falling leaves, wind or rain.
  • Use the built-in Tutorial and Help pages whenever you need them.

Why we built it

Many farmers still keep records in notebooks, where numbers are easy to lose and hard to add up. AgriNote turns that diary into a simple app on your phone, with clear screens that anyone can use.
