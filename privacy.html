Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information AgriNote collects, why we collect it and how it is used. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and login details, used to create and secure your account.
  • Farm records you enter: crop names, area, sowing dates, expenses, income and sale details.
  • App activity: basic activity such as when you use the app, so it can work properly and be improved.
  • Ad interactions: how many times a banner ad is shown or tapped.

2. How we use your information

  • To run the app, save your records and show your totals, profit and charts.
  • To keep your account safe and prevent misuse.
  • To send in-app notifications and show the app version or maintenance messages.
  • To show banner ads and count their impressions and clicks.
  • To fix problems and improve AgriNote.

3. Where your data is stored

Your account and records are stored using Google Firebase services. Images used for ads are hosted with Cloudinary. We use access rules to limit who can read your data, but no online service can promise perfect security.

4. Advertising

AgriNote may show banner ads from sponsors. Advertisers can only see total counts for their own banners, such as impressions and clicks. They do not receive your email address or your farm records. If you tap an ad, you may open a third-party website that has its own privacy policy.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with the service providers needed to run the app, such as Firebase, or when the law requires it.

6. Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your crops, expenses and income entries inside the app.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data by contacting us below.

7. Children

AgriNote is made for farmers and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from young children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its date.
