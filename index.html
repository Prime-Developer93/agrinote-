EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE

Everything your farm diary needs

Stop juggling notebooks and calculators. AgriNote keeps your farm's numbers simple, clear and always with you.

Crop Management

Add each crop with its area in acres and sowing date. See the days left or passed and its Active status at a glance.

Expense Tracking

Log every expense under the crop it belongs to. Your total spending is always one tap away.

Income Tracking

Record each sale with the market, date and amount. Every sale shows its share of that crop's income.

Profit at a Glance

Expenses, income and net profit for every crop, with simple bars that show where your money goes.

Farm Analytics

Financial trends, income and expense breakdowns, and a crop-wise profit comparison with margin.

Themes & Effects

Choose from 6 soft themes, including a dark Midnight Forest, and add falling leaves, wind or rain.
